Clips Uploader — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

What Clips Uploader is

Clips Uploader is a private, internal publishing tool. It is used by one person, the owner of the YouTube channels it manages, to upload his own short videos to his own channels and to answer comments under those videos. It has no other users, no public sign-up and no accounts for anyone else.

YouTube API Services

Clips Uploader uses YouTube API Services. By using it, the channel owner agrees to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What data the tool accesses

  • Our own channels: channel id and name, the ids, titles and public statistics (views, likes, comment counts) of the videos we uploaded.
  • Comments under our own videos: the public comment text, the commenter's public display name and the comment id. We read them only to reply as the channel, to avoid replying twice and to pass questions or requests (for example a takedown request) to the owner.

The tool does not access private data of viewers and does not read other channels' private data. Data is never sold and is not used for advertising.

One processor: to draft a reply, the text of a public comment (without the commenter's name) is sent to an AI writing service (Anthropic's Claude) and the draft comes back. A request to remove a video or fix a credit is forwarded, with the commenter's public name, to the owner's private messenger (Telegram) so that he can act on it the same day. Nothing else is shared with or transferred to third parties.

How the data is stored

Data is stored only on the owner's own computer. Public statistics of our own videos are kept for our internal dashboard. Comment texts and commenter names are deleted or refreshed within 30 days.

Revoking access

The channel owner can revoke the tool's access to any channel at any time on the Google security settings page. After revocation the tool can no longer read or upload anything for that channel. Stored data for that channel is deleted within 7 days of revocation.

Requests and deletion

Anyone who wants a comment-related record deleted, or a video removed or credited differently, can write a comment under the video or contact us at vlasvlasenko202@gmail.com. We act on such requests within one business day.
