Clips Uploader — Terms of Service

Last updated: 20 November 2026

1. What this is

Clips Uploader is a private, internal publishing tool. It is operated by one person and is used only by him to upload his own short videos to YouTube channels he owns and to answer comments under those videos. It is not offered to the public: there is no sign-up, no other users and nothing to buy.

2. YouTube Terms of Service

Clips Uploader uses YouTube API Services. Anyone who uses the tool agrees to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. The tool is used in line with the YouTube API Services Terms of Service and the YouTube API Services Developer Policies.

3. Who may use it

Only the owner of the connected YouTube channels. A channel is connected only by its owner, through Google's own consent screen, and can be disconnected by him at any time on the Google security settings page.

4. What the tool does

  • Uploads a video once, to one channel, with its own title and description, at a scheduled time.
  • Adds the uploaded video to a playlist of the same channel.
  • Reads public comments under our own videos and posts replies as the channel.
  • Reads public statistics (views, likes, comment counts) of our own videos for an internal dashboard.

The tool does not download YouTube videos, does not scrape YouTube, does not access other people's channels and does not sell or share YouTube data.

5. Content

The operator is responsible for the videos he uploads and for following YouTube's Community Guidelines and copyright rules. A request to remove a video or to correct a credit can be sent to the address below and is handled within one business day.

How data is accessed, stored and deleted is described in the Privacy Policy, which also links to the Google Privacy Policy.
